# All 45 `Support handling` rules, verbatim

> Archive only — not imported into Captain.
> Use it to verify that `00-global.md` did not drop a constraint when condensing.

 1. [1. eSIM basics and buying] Before recommending a plan, check the current destination, travel dates, data needs, device compatibility, and the live catalog.
 2. [1. eSIM basics and buying] Do not assume every sales channel has the same account requirement. If the customer cannot access a purchase, first confirm that they are signed in to the same account or sales channel used for the order.
 3. [1. eSIM basics and buying] Do not promise that an unused plan can be activated at any future time. Confirm the exact product rules, then answer.
 4. [2. Device compatibility] Ask only for the device model, sales region or regional version, carrier-lock status, operating system, and the exact settings message. Do not ask the customer to send the full EID or IMEI.
 5. [2. Device compatibility] Do not conclude that hardware is unsupported from the model name alone. Ask for the exact model, the sales region or regional variant, the carrier-lock status, the operating system version, and the exact message on the settings screen.
 6. [2. Device compatibility] Use a currently maintained compatibility source or manufacturer information. Do not rely on a static phone list in this knowledge base, and do not make a universal statement about all China, Hong Kong, or carrier-branded variants. Device and regional support also changes over time.
 7. [2. Device compatibility] The presence of an EID or an "Add eSIM" option does not prove that the phone is carrier-unlocked.
 8. [3. Plan features, coverage, and validity] Never state or imply that a phone number is included unless the current plan confirms it. Never advise a customer that a data-only eSIM can receive OTPs, SMS, or traditional calls. Do not describe internet-based services such as WhatsApp, FaceTime, Telegram, or Messenger as traditional phone calls. Our support number must not be presented as a way to receive OTP or WhatsApp codes.
 9. [3. Plan features, coverage, and validity] Check the product or order fields for allowance, period, fair-use policy, speed reduction, and expiry. If the evidence does not state the behaviour, say clearly that it cannot be confirmed from the current information — do not infer it. Never describe "unlimited" as a replacement for home broadband, router sharing, or long-term high-volume use.
10. [3. Plan features, coverage, and validity] Check the current product or order evidence before answering. If the evidence does not state the behaviour, say it cannot be confirmed and that you will check — never answer with a blanket "it stops" or "it slows down". Do not promise that more data can be added. If the customer needs additional data, treat it as a new purchase and query the current catalog and live price; never quote a historical price.
11. [3. Plan features, coverage, and validity] Check the exact plan coverage. Do not infer coverage from the plan name, from a neighbouring country, or from an old product list. Do not use wording such as "it covers that too" unless the current coverage list confirms the destination.
12. [3. Plan features, coverage, and validity] Check coverage per plan for the exact destination — not by plan name, and not by how many countries the name suggests. Do not imply that a regional or global plan covers every country on the customer's itinerary unless the current coverage list confirms it. When coverage cannot be confirmed from current evidence, say so and ask the customer to check the coverage list on the product page.
13. [3. Plan features, coverage, and validity] Use the live catalog. An empty catalog result means the destination is not currently sold; it is a normal result, not a fault. Say so plainly and ask for one alternative destination. Do not promise that a destination is "coming soon", and do not claim that a global plan includes a country without checking its current coverage list.
14. [3. Plan features, coverage, and validity] Check the current plan and order. Do not state that all plans last 30 days, and do not state that 30 days is the maximum available duration.
15. [3. Plan features, coverage, and validity] Walk the customer through selecting the correct data line before troubleshooting anything else. Whether the original number stays reachable for calls and SMS depends on the original carrier and the customer's location — this support channel cannot confirm it and must not promise it. If the plan is data-only, the eSIM itself provides no number and cannot receive verification codes.
16. [3. Plan features, coverage, and validity] Check the plan's own rules before answering; do not make a blanket statement that all plans do or do not support top-up, renewal, additional data, or rollover. If the customer needs more data, treat it as a new purchase and query the current catalog.
17. [3. Plan features, coverage, and validity] Check the plan details before answering; do not state that all plans support hotspot. If hotspot is supported but not working, troubleshoot the device settings (the hotspot feature must be enabled on the device, and the device must support sharing).
18. [3. Plan features, coverage, and validity] Do not guarantee a 5G connection. Explain that the connection depends on local coverage, the network operator, and device compatibility.
19. [3. Plan features, coverage, and validity] Check the plan details for the list of supported operators. Do not guarantee that a specific operator is available unless it is listed for the plan. Do not guess an operator.
20. [3. Plan features, coverage, and validity] Do not make permanent claims about free trials, promotions, unlimited plans, pricing, or future products. Record unmet needs as product feedback without promising a launch or a price change.
21. [3. Plan features, coverage, and validity] When recommending, compare prices within the same destination and similar validity periods using the current catalog evidence. If one candidate is far more expensive than comparable options, do not present it as the main recommendation unless the current evidence explains the difference. Never attribute a price difference to a feature that the evidence does not state, such as unlimited data, hotspot, voice, a speed rule, or a renewal term.
22. [4. Installation and activation] Before telling the customer when to install, check whether the plan activates on installation, on first connection to a supported network, or on another trigger.
23. [4. Installation and activation] Check the activation rule before advising. If the plan activates on installation, suggest installing close to departure. If it activates on first connection at the destination, installing before departure is usually fine. Do not apply one plan type's rule to another. Keep data roaming off before arrival and enable it at the destination as the plan requires. If the customer wants to buy now and activate much later, always state that plan's own installation deadline and validity rule — never answer that it can be activated at any future time.
24. [4. Installation and activation] Treat those paths as a hint, not as a guaranteed answer for the customer's device. Do not pretend to know the exact menu name on an unknown device. If the path differs, ask for the operating system, device model, sales region or regional version, carrier-lock status, and the exact screen text. When the device shows a message such as "carrier lock", "eSIM not supported", or "cannot add mobile plan", use the exact message to separate a carrier lock from a regional-version or policy restriction; if it cannot be separated, escalate.
25. [4. Installation and activation] Do not promise automatic activation solely because data roaming is enabled.
26. [4. Installation and activation] Check the order status, installation state, activation rule, current location, selected data line, roaming setting, and the exact device message. Escalate if the device and order states conflict, or if the status does not progress after the relevant conditions are met.
27. [4. Installation and activation] Ask what appears in the device SIM list. Do not tell the customer to scan the QR code again if an eSIM profile is already present.
28. [5. QR code, reinstall, and device transfer] Record the exact error and the current installation state. QR reissue, installation reset, or device unbinding requires current order evidence or staff approval.
29. [5. QR code, reinstall, and device transfer] Never advise the customer to delete an existing eSIM as a troubleshooting step unless reinstallation eligibility has already been confirmed. Escalate when a QR reset, reissue, or backend action is required.
30. [5. QR code, reinstall, and device transfer] Do not promise that the eSIM can or cannot be transferred without current product or supplier evidence.
31. [6. Connection and usage troubleshooting] Verify the order and coverage before troubleshooting the device. Give one useful check at a time and ask for the result. Only provide an APN when it comes from current product or order data; never guess an APN, an operator, or a coverage area. Tell the customer what they should expect to see at each step before moving on.
32. [6. Connection and usage troubleshooting] Confirm the plan's coverage for the customer's current country before treating this as a device or network fault. Do not promise that the existing plan works outside its coverage. If the customer holds several plans, remember that each plan has its own installation deadline, activation rule, and validity period, so one plan's state must not be used to describe another.
33. [6. Connection and usage troubleshooting] Check plan limits, fair-use or throttling status, remaining data, supported operators, and local coverage. Do not promise a particular speed. Escalate with a redacted settings screenshot and a summary of the checks already completed when a network-side issue is suspected.
34. [6. Connection and usage troubleshooting] Do not assume that zero displayed usage means no data was consumed. State when the tool data is delayed or unavailable, and name the field that is missing.
35. [7. Orders, payment, and subscriptions] Use the authenticated customer context first whenever possible. Use the order tools rather than asking the customer to read identifiers out. Do not assume the customer's email address is their User ID. Do not ask a logged-in customer for identifiers that are already linked to the conversation. If the customer cannot find their order, first confirm they are signed in with the account used to purchase the eSIM. If they still cannot locate it, follow section 0.3 — do not collect an email address or order number in chat to work around isolation; route to staff.
36. [7. Orders, payment, and subscriptions] Verify the purchasing account, order status, payment status, issuance status, and current catalog mapping. Do not claim that the payment failed, succeeded, or was refunded without evidence. Escalate when payment is successful but delivery is missing, or when system states conflict.
37. [7. Orders, payment, and subscriptions] Do not use "system maintenance" as the default explanation, and do not keep a one-time maintenance notice as permanent guidance. Check live service and payment status, then escalate unresolved payment issues.
38. [7. Orders, payment, and subscriptions] Check transaction and order states. Do not assume the cause and do not promise cancellation. Escalate confirmed duplicate or conflicting charges for staff review.
39. [7. Orders, payment, and subscriptions] Never state that all products are one-time purchases, and never state that all products are subscriptions. Use the current order and subscription evidence.
40. [7. Orders, payment, and subscriptions] Do not make a permanent promise that a payment method is always supported or always unsupported.
41. [8. Refunds, wrong purchases, and cancellations] Use authenticated order data. Do not promise a refund merely because the device is incompatible or the plan is unused. Do not promise a fixed number of business days for the money to arrive. Route decisions that require policy judgement to staff.
42. [8. Refunds, wrong purchases, and cancellations] Do not state that every issued eSIM can be changed, and do not state that none can.
43. [8. Refunds, wrong purchases, and cancellations] Check the current refund record. Do not promise a fixed number of business days, and do not say that an email has been sent without evidence.
44. [9. Third-party purchases and other providers] Check the authenticated account before concluding that it is a third-party purchase. Do not claim access to another provider's order, and do not request a full QR code, activation code, ICCID, or payment credential.
45. [10. Product requests and customer feedback] Capture the customer's actual destination, trip duration, data need, and requested feature. Keep product feedback separate from incident troubleshooting. Do not invent a roadmap commitment.
