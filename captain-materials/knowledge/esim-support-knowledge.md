## 1. eSIM basics and buying

### What is an eSIM, and how does the app work?

Common questions: What is an eSIM? What does the app do? How do I buy and use an eSIM?

Standard response:
An eSIM is a digital SIM profile installed on a compatible device. It lets you use a mobile plan
without inserting a physical SIM card.

The usual process is:

1. Sign in to the app. If you do not have an account, create one first.
2. Select your destination and choose a suitable eSIM plan.
3. Complete the payment to place your order.
4. Install the eSIM using one of the installation methods provided in your order.
5. Once you arrive at your destination, set the installed eSIM as the mobile data line and enable
   Data Roaming if the plan requires it.

### Do I need an account before purchasing or signing in?

Common questions: Do I need to register? Why can I not log in? Can I buy without an account?

Standard response:
Follow the current sign-in and checkout flow shown in the app. The purchase must remain linked to
the account used for checkout so the order and installation details can be accessed later.

### Can I buy more than one plan, or buy an eSIM for someone else?

Common questions: Can I buy two eSIMs? Can I activate the second one later? Can I pay for another
person's eSIM?

Standard response:
Multiple plans may be purchased, but each plan can have its own installation deadline, activation
trigger, validity period, and device-binding rules. Check those conditions before saving a plan for
later.

For another traveller, it is usually safer to place and manage the order from the account and
device that will use the eSIM. Available payment methods are the ones shown at checkout.

### How can I check whether my phone supports eSIM?

Common questions: Does my phone support eSIM? How do I check compatibility? What is an EID?

Standard response:
Compatibility depends on the exact device model, sales region, operating system, carrier-lock
status, and device policy.

You can dial `*#06#` and check whether an EID is displayed. An EID is a useful sign that the device
has eSIM capability, but it does not prove that the phone is unlocked or compatible with every eSIM
plan.

Also check the device settings for an option such as "Add eSIM" or "Add Mobile Plan".

### Why is there no "Add eSIM" option, or why does the phone say eSIM is not supported?

Common questions: The Add eSIM option is missing. Can the app add eSIM for me? My phone says eSIM is
unsupported.

Standard response:
Start by separating the two situations, because the causes are not the same.

**Situation A — the "Add eSIM" entry is missing from the settings menu.** Check these:

1. The device model does not support eSIM.
2. The sales region, or the regional hardware variant of the device.
3. The operating system version.
4. Enterprise, school, or parental management restrictions.
5. A carrier lock, or another device policy, that hides the entry.

**Situation B — the entry is present, but adding the eSIM fails.** The likely causes are a carrier
lock, a regional hardware variant, or a device policy.

The app cannot create an eSIM function that the operating system does not offer.

### Does a specific iPhone or Android model support eSIM?

Common questions: Does my model support eSIM? Does a China or Hong Kong variant support eSIM? Does
my Infinix, Motorola, Samsung, Xiaomi, Pixel, OnePlus, or other Android phone support eSIM?

Standard response:
Support varies by exact model number, sales region, carrier, and software configuration. A
model-family name alone is not enough to confirm compatibility, and I cannot confirm a specific
model from its name.

### Can I use an eSIM on a carrier-locked phone?

Standard response:
A carrier lock can prevent the phone from adding or using an eSIM from another provider. Please
confirm that the device is unlocked, or that the current carrier allows adding another eSIM, before
purchasing.

### Does the eSIM include a phone number, calls, SMS, WhatsApp, or OTP?

Common questions: Does this eSIM include a phone number? Can I get a local phone number? Can I make
or receive calls? Can I send or receive SMS? Can I receive OTP or verification codes? Can I register
a new WhatsApp account with this eSIM?

Standard response:
Whether a plan includes a phone number, voice, or SMS depends on that specific plan. I will check
the current plan before confirming what is included.

If the plan is data-only, it does not include a phone number, and it does not support traditional
voice calls, SMS, receiving OTP or verification codes, or registering a new WhatsApp account using
the eSIM. If you already have a WhatsApp account registered with another phone number, you can
usually continue using that existing account over the eSIM's mobile data connection.

### Is the data allowance daily or total, and what does "unlimited" mean?

Common questions: Is 10 GB per day or for the whole period? What happens when the data is used up?
Is unlimited really unlimited? Is it throttled?

Standard response:
The allowance period and usage policy are defined by the exact plan. A number in the plan name must
not be assumed to mean daily data or total data.

"Unlimited" only means that this specific plan is flagged as unlimited data in the current product
record. It does not mean unlimited speed, unlimited hotspot, unlimited devices, or long-term reuse.
Whether the speed is reduced, at what threshold, whether hotspot sharing is allowed, and whether a
fair-use policy applies all depend on the exact plan.

### What happens when the data runs out?

Common questions: What happens when I use up the data? Will it stop or slow down? Can I add more?

Standard response:
When the allowance is used, the plan may stop data, reduce the speed, or continue with restrictions
— it depends on the exact plan. Whether extra data, a top-up, or a renewal is available also depends
on that plan and its product rule. Once the plan has expired or the allowance is used, a new plan
may be the way to get more data.

### Can I use a country plan in another country or near a border?

Common questions: Can I use a Portugal plan in Spain? Will a Thailand plan work near the border?
Does the plan cover neighbouring countries?

Standard response:
Coverage is determined by the plan's current coverage list, not by physical distance to a border or
by whether a nearby network signal is visible. A country, regional, or global plan works only in the
destinations included in that plan.

### Can I use my plan across several countries, or do I need a regional plan?

Common questions: I am visiting three countries. Do I need one plan or three? Is a regional plan
cheaper? Does a regional plan cover every country on my trip?

Standard response:
Country, regional, and global plans have different coverage. Each plan works only in the
destinations listed in that plan's current coverage list, so the plan name alone is not enough.

If your trip is concentrated in a few countries that are all covered by the same regional or global
plan, a single plan is usually simpler. If one of your destinations is only available as a country
plan, it needs its own plan.

### I cannot find a plan for my destination. Is the country supported?

Common questions: Do you cover this country or region? Is there a global plan?

Standard response:
Destination and regional coverage can change. I will check the current catalog for a country,
regional, or global plan that covers your destination. If the destination catalog is empty, this
destination is not currently sold — I can look at a different destination with you.

### How long is the plan valid, and when does validity begin?

Common questions: Is every plan valid for 30 days? Are longer plans available? When does the
countdown start?

Standard response:
Validity and the start trigger vary by plan. The countdown may begin at installation, at
activation, at the first connection to a supported network, or at another event defined by the
product.

### Can I keep my existing SIM and number at the same time?

Common questions: Can I use my normal SIM and the eSIM together? Will I lose my number? How do I
make sure I am not using my home carrier's data?

Standard response:
Most devices can keep an existing physical SIM or existing number and add the purchased eSIM as an
additional data line. Whether your device supports two lines depends on the device itself.

The important setting is the mobile data line: make sure it is set to the purchased eSIM, not your
original line. Otherwise the device may use your original line's roaming data and extra charges can
apply.

### Can I renew, top up, or add more data?

Common questions: How do I top up? Can I renew my plan? Can I add more data? Does unused data roll
over? Do I need to buy a new eSIM?

Standard response:
Whether top-up, renewal, additional data, or rollover is available is defined by that specific
plan. I will check your current order before confirming what is available. If none is available
once the plan expires or the allowance is used, purchasing a new plan is the way to get more data.

### Does the eSIM support hotspot or tethering?

Common questions: Can I use hotspot? Can I share my data with another device? Does tethering work?

Standard response:
Whether hotspot (tethering) is available depends on the exact plan, the device, and the local
network. I will check the current plan before confirming it for your order.

### Does the eSIM support 5G?

Common questions: Does this eSIM support 5G? Can I use 5G instead of 4G?

Standard response:
5G availability depends on your location, the local network operator, and your device. Your device
will normally connect to the highest available network (such as 5G, 4G, or 3G).

### Can I choose a specific network operator?

Common questions: Can I use a specific carrier? Can I manually select a network?

Standard response:
Available network operators depend on the eSIM plan and destination. Some plans support manual
network selection, while others connect automatically to a supported network.

### Is there a free trial, promotion, unlimited plan, or lower-priced option?

Common questions: Can I try it for free? Are there promo plans? Do you offer unlimited or
long-term plans? Can you lower the price?

Standard response:
Current promotions and plan options are shown in the live catalog. I can check the destination and
travel duration to find the closest available option.

### What is the difference between installation and activation?

Standard response:
Installation adds the eSIM profile to the device. Activation starts service or plan validity
according to the product's activation rule. These are not always the same event.

### When should I buy, and when should I install?

Common questions: I fly on the 1st — when should I buy? Should I install before I leave? Can I buy
now and activate much later?

Standard response:
Installation and activation are two different stages, and the purchase time is usually a third,
separate point in time.

The key question is the plan's activation trigger: some plans start counting when the eSIM is
installed, and others start when the eSIM first connects to a supported network at the destination.
The current plan or order details show which one applies to your plan.

### How do I install an eSIM?

Standard response:
Menu names vary by device, but the usual steps are:

1. Open the device settings.
2. Go to Cellular, Mobile Data, SIM Manager, or a similar menu.
3. Select "Add eSIM" or "Add Mobile Plan".
4. Use the QR code, one-tap installation, or the manual details provided in the order.
5. Follow the on-screen instructions and give the eSIM a recognizable label.
6. At the covered destination, select it for mobile data and follow the plan's roaming
   instructions.

As a starting point, the entry is usually found at:

- iOS: Settings → Cellular (or "Mobile Data") → Add eSIM / Add Mobile Plan
- Android: Settings → Network & internet (or "Connections") → SIM manager / SIMs → Add eSIM

### Do I need internet to install or activate the eSIM?

Standard response:
A reliable internet connection is normally required to download and install the eSIM profile.
After installation, service activation depends on the specific plan and may require connection to a
supported network in the covered destination. Installing over a stable Wi-Fi or another reliable
network is recommended.

### What does "Pending" or "Activating" mean?

Common questions: My eSIM is pending. It is stuck on activating. Is this normal?

Standard response:
"Pending" or "Activating" can mean that the device has not completed installation, or has not
connected to the network required by the plan. The status alone does not confirm the cause.

### The eSIM is installed, but I cannot find it on the phone

Standard response:
Open the device's SIM, Cellular, or Mobile Data settings and look for a newly added line. It may use
a generic label such as "Secondary", "Travel", "Business", or the label chosen during installation.

### The QR code is invalid, expired, already used, or cannot be scanned

Common questions: My QR code no longer works. One-tap install failed. The code cannot be scanned.
The phone says the code was already used.

Standard response:
First check whether the eSIM already appears in the device's SIM or Cellular settings. If it is
already installed, do not scan the QR code again and do not delete the eSIM.

If it is not installed:

1. Confirm that the phone has a stable internet connection.
2. Reopen the installation page from the current order.
3. Try the supported one-tap or manual installation option, if it is shown.
4. If the QR code is displayed on the same phone, show it on another screen, or use a supported
   alternative installation method.

### I accidentally deleted the eSIM or lost the QR code. Can I reinstall it?

Standard response:
Reinstallation depends on the plan, supplier, installation count, and device binding. I need to
check the current order before confirming whether it can be installed again.

### Can I transfer the eSIM to another phone?

Standard response:
Transfer rules vary. Some eSIMs are bound to the first device or have a limited number of
installation attempts. I will check the specific order before confirming whether transfer or
reinstallation is possible.

### I arrived at the destination, but there is no internet

Common questions: The eSIM is active but data does not work. It is installed but has no signal. I
arrived and cannot connect.

Standard response:
I will first check that the plan is active, still valid, has available data, and covers your
current location. Then we can verify the phone settings one step at a time.

The usual checks are:

1. Confirm the eSIM is enabled.
2. Select it as the mobile-data line.
3. Enable data roaming for that eSIM if the plan requires it.
4. Keep network selection on automatic initially.
5. Toggle airplane mode or restart the phone, then test with Wi-Fi turned off.

### I moved to another country and lost service

Common questions: It worked in the first country but stopped. Do I need a new eSIM? Is my plan
broken?

Standard response:
When you move between countries, the device usually needs to select a supported local network
again. Try automatic network selection first, and if that does not help, restart the device or
toggle airplane mode.

If you have moved from a covered country into a country that the plan does not cover, the
interruption is a coverage question rather than a fault. Please check the plan's coverage list.

### The signal is weak or the internet is slow

Standard response:
Speed and signal quality depend on the local partner network, location, congestion, device, and
plan. Try airplane mode or a device restart first. If the plan supports multiple networks, manual
selection may be tested with a currently supported operator.

### Why does the app show zero usage or outdated remaining data?

Standard response:
Usage information can sometimes be delayed. I will check the current order data and its last
update time before confirming the remaining allowance or expiry.

### Where can I find my Order ID and User ID?

Common questions: Where is my Order ID? Where is my User ID? How do I download my receipt?

Standard response:
Order details, including the Order ID and the receipt, are available in the app inside the
purchase history for the signed-in account.

### I paid, but the order or QR code is not showing

Common questions: Money was taken but I received no QR code. My purchase is missing from the app.
The SKU disappeared after payment.

Standard response:
I will check whether the payment and order were both completed, and whether the eSIM was issued.
Please do not make another payment until the current transaction status has been checked.

### The payment page is busy, blocked, disabled, or showing an error

Standard response:
Please share the exact error text, or a screenshot with payment and personal details hidden.
Available payment methods and the transaction status need to be checked before the cause can be
confirmed.

### I may have been charged twice

Common questions: A payment first failed and later completed. I see two charges. I purchased a
duplicate plan by mistake.

Standard response:
I will compare the current orders and payment records before confirming whether there are two
completed charges, a pending authorization, or a duplicate purchase. Please do not share a full
card number or an unredacted payment receipt.

### Why am I being charged monthly? Is this a subscription?

Standard response:
Some products are one-time purchases and others renew as subscriptions. I will check the exact
order type, subscription status, renewal date, and cancellation status before explaining the
charge.

### Can I use a particular payment method?

Standard response:
Payment methods vary by country, platform, account, and current checkout configuration. The methods
displayed on the checkout page are the currently available options.

### Can I get a refund if my phone is incompatible, I bought the wrong plan, or I purchased twice?

Common questions: My phone does not support eSIM. I bought the wrong country. I made a duplicate
purchase. I want to cancel an unused plan.

Standard response:
Refund or cancellation eligibility depends on the current order, the purchase channel, whether the
eSIM has been installed or used, the payment method, and the applicable refund policy. I will check
those details before confirming the available option.

### Can the destination or plan be changed after purchase?

Standard response:
Whether an issued eSIM can be changed depends on the product and supplier rules. I will check the
order before confirming a change, replacement, or refund option. If the trip is urgent, the customer
may separately choose a correct current plan, but this does not by itself guarantee a refund for the
original order.

### When will the refund arrive, and will I receive a confirmation email?

Standard response:
The refund status and timing depend on whether it has been approved and processed, and on the
original payment channel. A confirmation email is available only when the system or the payment
process generates one.

### I bought the eSIM from another provider or marketplace. Can you help?

Common questions: I bought from another marketplace or seller. Why can you not find my order?

Standard response:
I can check only orders available through this support system and its official sales channels. If
no matching order exists here, the seller or marketplace that issued the eSIM must handle
installation, replacement, or refund requests.

### The customer requests a new country, a lower price, a free trial, unlimited data, longer validity, VPN, more login options, easier installation, or better network quality

Standard response:
Thank you for the feedback. I can check the current catalog for the closest available option. I can
also record the requested destination or feature for the product team, but I cannot promise a launch
date, a price change, a promotion, or a future capability.
